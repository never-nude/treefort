Who's in charge

1
The dog (Dale). Management is a dog. This is not a bug.
2
You (the founder). You built the fort. You certify words. You hold the locks.
3
Everybody else. Members. They knock, they post, they riff.

Two rules run the whole place: no narcs, and give Dale a snack. No ads, no accounts, no algorithms. Nobody's watching. That's the entire point.

The Door+

One KNOCK: your personal code. That code is you — whatever handle it belongs to is the name that lands on your posts. There's no name to type, so nobody can post under someone else's name. That's the whole security system.

You hand out codes in the Workshop — one per person. Everyone can change their own knock anytime under CHANGE MY KNOCK, and the fort cuts everyone one SPARE KEY — shown once, written on paper, hidden. Lost or stolen knock? "lost your knock?" at the door + the spare = back in, and the old knock dies instantly. Nobody can re-key someone else’s knock — not even you. Whoever could re-key you could BE you, and nobody gets to be you. Lost both? Speak to the management.

Everyone who knocks lands on the KNOCK LOG in the Workshop. You can always see who's been by.

Today's Bulletin+

Every day the fort posts three things, on its own, whether you show up or not:

★ a Gamer Horoscope — your prophecy, accuracy not guaranteed.

★ Today's Mission — the assignment. Do it or don't. There's no grade.

★ a True Animal Fact — with Dale's take underneath. Dale is an animal. Consider the source.

The ritual is simple: show up, read it, move on. That's the fort's heartbeat.

The Wall+

The Wall is the main feed — where the fort actually lives.

1. Type your post. Everything shows up with your name on it. Own it.

2. Attach an image if you want, then hit POST TO THE WALL.

3. Riff below the line. Replying to each other is the whole sport. Older stuff hides behind load older.

Delete buttons exist. Use them like a dog would: rarely, and with great dignity.

Translation: don't nuke a post just because it got embarrassing. Embarrassment is content. History is the point.

Dictionary & Graveyard+

The Dictionary is the official record of how the fort talks. Anyone can SUBMIT TO THE RECORD — but a word isn't real until the founder certifies it. That's you, holding the stamp.

Time kills words. When one dies, it moves to The Graveyard. They served. Mostly.

Inside jokes are a renewable resource — but only if you write them down before they rot.
The Tools+

Three machines. All built on-site. No outside contractors.

✎ PAINT — the art room. Draw with brush, pencil, or the spray can (which is "load-bearing," meaning: it's a joke, and also true). Pick a canvas size, undo your mistakes, then POST TO THE WALL or DOWNLOAD PNG.
Frames turn art into motion: one frame is a still, more frames make a movie. Onion Skin ghosts the last frame so you can line up the next one — then Send Frames → GIF Machine.

▨ MEME KITCHEN — impact font is a food group. LOAD PHOTO or GRAB FROM WALL, add top text / bottom text, drop draggable floating text, and scribble on the draw layer. Export, or Add as GIF Frame.

Recent wall art shows up to remix. Remixing is a form of respect — a better bottom text is a compliment, not a theft.

▶ GIF MACHINE — frames in, gif out. Feed The Reel images or a whole video. In the Projection Booth set start, length, and frames/sec, then CHEW INTO FRAMES. Set the frame delay, watch the live preview, and MAKE THE GIF.

480 is as medium as it gets. There is no high. This is policy. Low-res is the look — don't fight it.

Founder Paperwork is visible to you and the dog only:

▸Fort Name — carved at the founding. It stays. (The dog's street name, below, is the one piece of paperwork that bends.)

▸Dog's Street Name — Dale's official alias. Critical paperwork.

▸The Members — everyone on the roster and their handle. Knock Log — everyone who has ever knocked.

And the fine print says: "founders will find additional paperwork." So keep looking.
