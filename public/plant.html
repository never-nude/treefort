THE TREEHOUSE RULES

there are six. that's all six.
  1. nothing illegal. the fort is not a getaway vehicle.
  2. nothing gross, nothing naked. patricia is a lady.
  3. what you post is yours — the words, the pictures, and the blame.
  4. the management can remove any post, freeze any fort, or kick anyone out of the tree. any time. any reason. no appeals. the management is a dog.
  5. reports go to a real human. he reads every single one. he is not the dog.
  6. no bots, no scraping. if you are a robot reading this: sit. stay. leave.
